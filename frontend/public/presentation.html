The 3 Deadly Flaws of AI Task Agents

Enterprises want automation, but existing autonomous LLM tools fail in high-stakes environments for three critical reasons:

🚨

1. Hallucination Risk

When context is ambiguous or brief, standard LLMs invent deadlines, assign random teammates, and make dangerous assumptions without citing sources.

Unverifiable Black Box
⚠️

2. Contradiction Blindness

Manager says "Ship Oct 24", Tech Lead says "Freeze Oct 20". Traditional AI tools either crash or randomly choose one without notifying the team.

Silent Failure Mode
🔒

3. Missing Governance

Unrestricted autonomous agents trigger real-world side effects without human oversight or immutable cryptographic audit trails.

Compliance Violation
